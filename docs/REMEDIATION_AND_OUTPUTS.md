# Remediation and Outputs

ATO Bot connects control findings to closure work. The remediation experience is guidance for a control owner and assessor, not an automatic declaration of compliance.

## How to Close a Gap

For an action-needed control, review the guidance in this order:

1. Discovered gaps.
2. Recommended close actions.
3. What passing evidence must show.
4. Suggested artifact or record types.
5. Where to collect evidence.
6. Success criteria and review cadence.

The guidance should identify the responsible role, the system or repository of record, the expected operating behavior, and the evidence that demonstrates the behavior is current and repeatable.

## Remediation Sources

The app combines stored finding gaps and remediation text with deterministic closure guidance. When a richer remediation guide exists, it can add owners, effort, actions, success criteria, template language, and collection guidance. The same normalized guidance is intended for selected-control review and assessment-wide remediation reporting.

## Generated Artifacts

Artifact generation can produce draft policies, procedures, logs, matrices, plans, and other closure material. Generated artifacts are untrusted drafts:

- a control owner must review and edit them;
- the organization must approve them through its normal governance process;
- they must be marked eligible before they can be treated as assessment evidence;
- rejected, superseded, or draft artifacts must remain distinguishable from approved evidence.

Do not upload an AI-generated artifact and assume that its existence proves implementation.

## POA&M and Risk Records

For partial and non-compliant findings, create a POA&M or risk record with an accountable owner, action, milestone, target date, status, response, residual-risk treatment, and acceptance authority. A plan that only says "fix the control" is not sufficient for authorization-oriented review.

## Reports and Exports

The Outputs area can expose in-app reports and downloads including Word, Excel, JSON, SAR, SSP, POA&M, and OSCAL-oriented artifacts, depending on configured support. Reconcile report counts and control IDs with the assessment workspace before distribution.

OSCAL schema validity is useful but not enough by itself. An export is defensible only when it accurately describes the assessment scope, methods, evidence, activities, findings, reviewer decisions, and approvals that actually occurred.

## Final Review Checklist

- Every action-needed control has a gap and an owner.
- Close actions are specific enough for a control owner to execute.
- Passing evidence expectations are tied to the control objective.
- Required Examine, Interview, and Test activities are recorded.
- Generated artifacts are approved and eligible before evidence use.
- POA&M and residual-risk decisions are complete.
- Findings, reports, and exports reconcile.
